Embalenhle Family Home

Four-bedroom house with a double garage. The front is single storey under a lean-to roof; the back is double storey under a skillion roof. Both roofs are IBR sheeting. A ground-floor bedroom suite lets your parents live without using the stairs.

Truss T2, lean-to (9 no.)

  • Mono-pitch nail-plated timber trusses at 1175 centres, 9 per roof. Preliminary members: 38 × 114 chords and 38 × 76 webs, SA pine. The truss supplier designs and certifies the trusses, nail plates and bracing under SANS 10243 using the loads and reactions in Part 10 of the calculation pad.
  • T1 spans 8500 from the back wall to the front (junction) wall, wall plate +5.70, 300 heel at the back, 500 eaves overhang at the back and 300 at the front. T2 spans 6000 from the street wall (plate +2.65) to a 50 × 152 ledger on the junction wall face (+2.65), 300 heel, 500 overhang at the street.
  • Purlins 38 × 114 on edge at every top-chord panel point (1214 on plan for T1, 1200 for T2), plus one at each overhang tip. Fix each purlin to each truss with a galvanised purlin cleat rated for at least 2.4 kN uplift.
  • Hold-down: one 30 × 1.6 galvanised strap at every truss end, built into the wall at least 13 courses (T1) and 8 courses (T2) below the plate. Ledger: M12 chemical anchors at 600 centres.
  • Ceiling: 6.4 gypsum board on 38 × 38 brandering at 400, 135 glass-wool insulation, fixed to the bottom chords. Diagonal top-chord and web bracing to the supplier's layout.
