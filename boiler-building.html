Problem statement

The project was abandoned after the previous contractor cast the concrete surface bed in the wrong direction. The original design also had no crawl beam, so there was no way to hoist the boilers in and out.

Scope

What I was asked to deliver

As-built drawings of the boiler house as it stands, plus the design of a 2-ton crawl beam for hoisting the boilers.

Solution

How I solved it

  1. Drew the as-built structure to match what was on site: UC254 × 254 × 107 columns on the existing 600 × 600 plinths, facebrick to +4.016 m with perforated plate above, and an 11° mono-pitch Kliplok roof on 100 mm C-purlins and light steel trusses.
  2. Reconfigured the bracing (Rev 2).
  3. Designed a 2-ton crawl beam, a UC203 × 203 × 46 with its connections to the frame, for hoisting the boilers (Rev 3).
  4. Issued plan, four elevations, structural layout, four grid elevations and connection details.
