Problem statement

The client wanted a new double-storey family home on a 526 m² subdivided portion, within the building lines (3 m for double storey, 2 m for single storey).

Scope

What I was asked to deliver

Council submission drawings for Eidetic Designs: site plan, floor plans, four elevations, section, roof plan, staircase and services.

Solution

How I solved it

  1. Drew equal 129.32 m² floors stacked over each other to keep the footprint within the building lines.
  2. Put the double garage, office/study, guest bedroom and open lounge and dining on the ground floor, around a double-volume stair.
  3. Put the master suite with walk-in closet, a second bedroom, a pyjama lounge and balconies on the first floor.
  4. Showed compliance: coverage 178.71 m², FAR 0.61, and annual energy 3.56 kWh/m² against 5.00 allowed.
