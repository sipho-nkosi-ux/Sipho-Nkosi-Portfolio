Problem statement

The owners wanted a garage and extra living space behind their existing 275 m² house, within the 532 m² coverage limit in a cold climatic zone (zone 1).

Scope

What I was asked to deliver

Council submission drawings for Eidetic Designs: a new two-storey outbuilding, including a full roof-truss construction sheet.

Solution

How I solved it

  1. Stacked the garage, kitchen and lounge under two bedrooms and a bathroom to save footprint (148.92 m² outbuilding).
  2. Put the straight-flight stair in a glazed stair tower.
  3. Kept total coverage at 424.10 m² against 532.00 m² allowed.
  4. Showed compliance: annual energy 4.52 kWh/m² against 5.00 allowed, and lighting load 370 W against 745 W allowed. Drew the truss construction sheet.
