Problem statement

The client wanted to turn a corner property into student accommodation for 43 occupants, keeping the existing swimming pool.

Scope

What I was asked to deliver

Council submission drawings for Eidetic Designs: site plan, ground and first floor plans, four elevations, section, roof plan, services and details.

Solution

How I solved it

  1. Drew two-storey additions with bedrooms and study rooms off double-loaded corridors, shared bathrooms, a kitchen and dining room, and an entertainment room, arranged around the existing pool.
  2. Showed a separate caretaker flat for on-site management.
  3. Drew a hipped 26° concrete-tile roof on timber trusses.
  4. Showed compliance: annual energy 9.09 kWh/m² against 12.50 allowed, and glazing below 15% of net floor area.
