Problem statement

The client wanted 32 student units on an irregular 763.90 m² stand, meeting the H2 boarding-house requirements and the SANS 10400-XA energy limits.

Scope

What I was asked to deliver

Council submission drawings for BlueLid Architects: site plan, floor plans, elevations, sections, roof plan, services layouts and Part XA calculations.

Solution

How I solved it

  1. Drew a long two-storey block angled to follow the stand boundary, fitting 32 self-contained units on two floors.
  2. Gave the units access from open walkways on steel columns, with a stair at each end.
  3. Showed a laundry room and an electrical control room, and a light 5° mono-pitch metal roof.
  4. Showed compliance: annual energy 9.10 kWh/m² against 12.50 allowed, and glazing below 15% of net floor area.
